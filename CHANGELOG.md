# Changelog

All notable changes to **AdCopyCraft AI** are documented in this file.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- **Multi-Platform Ad Generation Engine**: Algorithmic copy synthesis across Google Search RSA (15 headlines $\le 30$ chars, 4 descriptions $\le 90$ chars, sitelinks, callouts), Google Display, LinkedIn Sponsored Content, LinkedIn InMail, and Meta B2B ads.
- **Demographic Targeting Matrix**: Tailored angles for C-Suite, VP/Head, Director, and Engineering Managers across Cloud Infra, Cybersecurity, AI/MLOps, FinTech, and Supply Chain.
- **3-Angle A/B/C Copy Variation**: Pain-Point & Risk Mitigation, Outcome & ROI Acceleration, and Authority & Social Proof.
- **Editorial Compliance Engine & 1-Click Auto-Fix**: Audits Google Ads and LinkedIn policies (character limits, no exclamations in search headlines, no shouty all-caps, no clickbait). Auto-sanitizes copy to word boundaries.
- **Predictive Performance Telemetry**: Google Ad Strength score (0-100), predicted CTR % with quantifier lift modeling, and Flesch-Kincaid readability scoring.
- **Omnichannel Bulk Export Center**: RFC 4180 CSV export for Google Ads Editor, LinkedIn Campaign CSV, Markdown brief, and JSON manifest.
- **4D Demographic Topology Canvas**: Mathematical $w(t) = \sin(t \cdot \omega)$ spatial particle system with reduced-motion compliance.
- **Enterprise RBAC**: Roles for `copywriter`, `campaign_manager`, and `admin` with live persona switcher.
- **Observability**: Prometheus metrics (`/api/metrics`) and Kubernetes liveness/readiness probes.
- **Comprehensive Automated Test Suite**: 35 tests covering all engines and API endpoints with 100% pass rate.
